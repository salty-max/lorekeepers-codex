The Windshapers are the shen'dorei who keep faith with the wind spirits. They descend from the first of their people to receive the spirits' gift of skysight, and they tended the instruments and constructs the spirits left on the island. They have sworn to repay that old debt: to brave the elemental plane, find their missing mentors and bring them home. Their Grand Skyseer, Ayessa Dawnsinger, believes the way lies below, in Azeroth, and the shamans of the Earthen Ring have stood with them.

They are not a forgiving people. To the cult's violence Ayessa answered blood for blood, and the Windshapers say of themselves that they neither forgive nor forget. They want no peace with the Al'Aketh, and they hold the High Order's magic at their sacred sites to be a danger to an island already coming apart.
