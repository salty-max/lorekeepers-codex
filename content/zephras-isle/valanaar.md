Valanaar is the capital of Zephras Isle, on its south-eastern edge, and the seat of its High Elder. The skycutters put in at its docks: small flying ships that once ran between the islands of the shen'dorei and now, for the first time, go down to Azeroth, to Dalaran for the High Order and to Mulgore for the Windshapers.

The shen'dorei's own account of their rebellion in Eldre'Thalas is not kept in the capital. It survives in four parts, kept in more than one place on the island, and one of them is in Falaath Village, which the Al'Aketh now hold. I know of nothing older that speaks for the shen'dorei, and I would give a good deal to have all four.
