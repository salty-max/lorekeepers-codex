Zephras Isle is an island that flies. It hangs in Skywall, the elemental plane of air, where the wind spirits carried the shen'dorei long ago, and it is home to the Skyborne who have lately come down to us. Its people live in Valanaar on the south-eastern edge, in the villages of the Shen'dar Highlands and at Thendal, and on the farms of the Gustberry Lowlands.

The spirits who bound the island left it some centuries ago. Their great anchor pylons still hold Zephras up, but their magic grows unsteady, and Ban'aethal began to fall into the sky one night and had to be emptied before morning. No skycutter has crossed to the other islands, Anvilas and Siroccas, for some time, and there are shen'dorei who fear they are all that is left of their people.

I have never stood there, traveller, and I envy you a little. Do keep away from the edges.
