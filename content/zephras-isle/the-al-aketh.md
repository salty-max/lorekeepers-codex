The Al'Aketh are a cult of the shen'dorei, known by their grey robes and shaved heads, who worship Al'Akir the Windlord as the rightful ruler of Skywall. Their preachers tell a simple story: the shen'dorei grew proud and took the bounty of the winds for granted, and the spirits abandoned them for it. On an island that is coming apart, it finds listeners.

They hold Falaath Village in the Shen'dar Highlands, and the Shrine of Akir under their High Priestess, Lorthuna. They have struck at the pylon watchtowers, fallen on the druids of the Nightclaw for a pylon of their own, sent spies among the villages and found at least one traitor in Valanaar. A former peacekeeper, Belguilos, called the Breaker, commands their fighters.
