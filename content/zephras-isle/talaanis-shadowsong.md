Talaanis Shadowsong is High Elder of Zephras Isle, and his people speak well of him. A good man, they say, and well loved, which the League has learned not to take for granted in a ruler. He governs from Valanaar with the island's other elders, Aetheen of the Gales among them.

His years in office have not been easy ones. The pylons are failing, the two orders of his people can barely agree on anything, and the Al'Aketh cult struck the western watchtower and killed his own nephew, a peacekeeper. When the cult's high priestess was at last driven out, he did not call it a victory. The wards that had hidden Zephras from Al'Akir were down, and he could only say that it might be years, even decades, before the Windlord turned his eye on them.
