Dalaran was the city of mages, seat of the Kirin Tor, the greatest school of magic in the human kingdoms, and its spires stood over Lordamere Lake for centuries. In the last war the Scourge took it, and the demon Archimonde laid it waste; its archmage, Antonidas, died with it.

[classic] The Kirin Tor are rebuilding. They have raised a great dome of violet light over the ruins, and no one passes through it without their leave. What they are doing inside, they keep to themselves; their wizards are seen in Hillsbrad and Silverpine, at the old internment camp and at Ambermill, and they are always busy. Archmage Ansirem Runeweaver speaks for them outside the dome, and speaks carefully.

[forever] The Kirin Tor were rebuilding behind a dome of violet light, and they lowered it sooner than they meant to: the city is not finished, and something has gone wrong inside it. Archmage Modera, who sends her image to the shore of Lordamere Lake, says the council has closed the city to outsiders while the disruption lasts, more concerned to keep up appearances than to prevent a catastrophe; she does not share their view. Ansirem Runeweaver speaks of magic all across Azeroth disturbed, as if a stone had been dropped into still water. The Skyborne of the High Order have come to them for help, and found them in the middle of their own trouble.
