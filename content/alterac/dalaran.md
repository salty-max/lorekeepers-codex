Dalaran's spires stood beside Lordamere Lake for centuries, home to the Kirin Tor and to generations of students of magic. In the Third War the Scourge took the city and killed Antonidas. Archimonde then destroyed what its defenders had failed to save.

[classic] The surviving Kirin Tor have raised a great dome of violet light over the ruins and begun rebuilding. Their wizards still work in Hillsbrad and Silverpine, at the old internment camp and Ambermill; Archmage Ansirem Runeweaver receives travellers outside the dome. There is something heartening in a city beginning again, even when its builders insist on drawing the curtains.

[forever] The rebuilding has been overtaken by a crisis within the city. Something has disrupted its ley lines, leaving its constructs erratic and destructive. The council wants the trouble kept private; Modera, whose image waits beside Lordamere Lake, has called for outside aid. The mages have survived one city's destruction. She is unwilling to let their pride help bring about another.
