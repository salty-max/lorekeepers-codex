The Suntara stones were a digger's rumour until Dorius Stonetender went into the Searing Gorge after them. A traveller found him on the road out, badly hurt and begging to be taken home to Ironforge. He was shot before they got there. He left behind a letter to his brother, Curator Thorius of the Hall of Explorers. The stones were real, it said, the Dark Irons had them, and their leader, Lathoric the Black, was using their power to enslave Dorius's excavation team. The diggers had been taken to the Cauldron to work night and day on something called Obsidion. Free them, the letter asked. It was signed as the last wish of a dead man.

Thorius dried his eyes and vowed that his brother would have his wish. The diggers were found in the Slag Pit, still labouring after their bodies had given out, held to the work by the stones until a relic guarded by the Twilight's Hammer was laid on the altar of Suntara. One of them lived long enough to say that it had all been a trap, and to ask that the man behind it be killed, for Thorius's sake.

The head of Lathoric the Black, when it was carried back to the Hall, was Dorius's. He had staged his own death on the road, and the letter with it. Thorius blamed the Firelord and the Old Gods, whose hold on that country is still strong: if a dwarf as honourable as his brother could be turned to the will of Ragnaros, he said, no one was safe.

Read the letter again knowing who wrote it, traveller. Nearly every line of it is true. Only the signature lies.
