Dalaran has nine pieces of its own, in its streets, the Eventide, the Fel Grove, the sewers and the spire. A city of mages keeps many rooms and many tempers, and its music changes with them. Its ley lines have gone wrong lately, and its own constructs have turned on it. Archmage Modera is the one who let the rest of the world know.
