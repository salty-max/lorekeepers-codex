Thendal Village, Thendal Grove and the Thendal Standing Stones share a tune. The village keeps its memory high up in a great tree, and the music is as quiet as an archive. The island once had to empty a whole village in a single night, and Thendal has not forgotten it. Of all the archives I know, that tree is the hardest to reach, and I would still make the climb.
