Shadowgale Forest, Falaath Village and the Ruins of Ban'aethal share a tune, and none of the three is a place to settle. The music is uneasy because the places are. The Al'Aketh have made Falaath their own. Ban'aethal stands empty, and the rest of the island lives by its warning.
