The Gustberry Lowlands, with the Windfield Orchard, the Fairweather Stables and the East Pylon Watchtower, have a tune of their own. These are the island's farms, and their music has the ease of a place where the work is done in daylight. The watchtower is the exception. It watches one of the anchor pylons, and on Zephras nobody treats the pylons lightly.
