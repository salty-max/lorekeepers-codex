The elementals are the oldest soldiers in the world. They served the Old Gods before the Titans came, and when their masters were chained beneath the earth they were cast out to a plane of their own, where the lords of fire, earth, air and water carry on the war among themselves. What a traveller meets on this side has usually slipped through, been dragged through or been left behind: rock that walks in the Badlands, a whirlwind loose in the Needles or the sands of Silithus, the restless water of the Stranglethorn coast.

Their courts are as fond of rank as any king's, with princes, dukes and barons. Ragnaros the Firelord was called into the world by a Dark Iron thane and has not left it. He consumed the essence of Prince Thunderaan of the air, and his lieutenants keep what is left of the prince in bindings. The servants of Neptulon the Tidehunter hate fire above all things: Duke Hydraxis makes allies of mortals from his island off Azshara, and an envoy of the Tribunal of the Tides at Sun Rock Retreat pays for every fire elemental put out in Stonetalon's Charred Vale. In Maraudon lives Princess Theradras, an elemental force of earth that the druids call kin to the Old Gods. In Silithus, the Twilight's Hammer summons elemental nobles and takes its orders from them.

Some are past serving anyone. The plague twisted the water elementals the Waterlords sent into the Eastern Plaguelands until they were sick and furious, and Duke Hydraxis calls it an affront to their purity. Shamans speak to the elements and are answered. The rest of us, traveller, are generally answered with a fist of stone.
