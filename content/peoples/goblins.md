Goblins turn up wherever there is money to be made, which is to say nearly everywhere: keeping the neutral ports, digging and logging for the Venture Company, selling explosives to anyone with the coin. Their homeland is the island of Kezan in the southern seas, and their capital is Undermine, where the trade princes who rule them do their business.

The island was not always theirs. Ask the Zandalar trolls, and you will hear that Kezan was once a grand territory of the Zandalar Empire, until, as they put it, the taint of the goblin trade princes forced them out. The longer account is that the trolls set goblins to dig a strange ore, kaja'mite, out of the island's volcano, and that the ore sharpened the diggers' wits until they took Kezan for themselves. I cannot vouch for the ore. I can believe the rest.

In the Second War the goblins sold the Horde sappers and zeppelins, and were paid for both. By the Third War they had stopped choosing sides, and their merchants, laboratories and shipyards would serve anyone. That arrangement suits them still. The Steamwheedle Cartel keeps its ports open to Alliance and Horde alike, the Venture Company strips whatever land no one stops it stripping, and a goblin engineer favours the kind of device that ends in a large bang. Gnomeregan's engineers call that a difference of philosophy. I suspect it is a difference of patience.
