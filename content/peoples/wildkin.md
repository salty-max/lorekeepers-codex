The great owl-bears of the north are called wildkin, or moonkin, and the night elves treat them with a reverence travellers do not always share. They are rumoured to have been made by Elune herself, and some say they were her pets. A night elf scholar who studies their feathers finds a magic in them that the creatures do not wield so much as carry, part of their very nature.

They are capable of great gentleness and of great ferocity, especially when something they hold important is threatened, and in Winterspring they are said to guard secrets of the night elves. Some there have gone mad, the reports say, from too long near the very thing they protect.

Lately the gentle ones have grown fewer. The moonkin of Darkshore are drawn to a great red crystal in the eastern hills, and the Sentinels of Auberdine no longer think them harmless. I would give any owl-bear a wide berth, traveller, and a mad one a wider.
