Torwa Pathfinder, a tauren camped in Un'Goro, has a score to settle with Lar'korwi, the great raptor who rules the crater's ravasaurs. Long ago, Torwa says, a brother of his helped himself to a carcass Lar'korwi had brought down, never knowing the raptor was watching from the trees. Lar'korwi stalked him for days and wrecked whatever he had touched. At last the brother tried to flee the crater, and Lar'korwi killed him not far from where Torwa now waits.

Torwa's plan is to make the beast come to him. Whoever steals meat from a fresh kill will be followed, as his brother was. The scent glands of Lar'korwi's mates, taken among the ravasaur eggs, make a lure, and the stolen meat, rubbed with it and left on a mountain trail among the bones of great creatures long gone, should draw him out. Torwa describes a massive beast with claws to rival any blade, and promises death to whoever meets his eyes.

Those who bring back his head are praised in the Earth Mother's name, and Torwa ranks the deed with those of his greatest ancestors. The plan was Torwa's. The stealing, the gathering and the fighting he left to whoever he could talk into it.
