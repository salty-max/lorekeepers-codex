Stormwind says its king is missing. A few people know a little more, and the trail they followed began, of all places, in a cheese shop. Bishop DeLavey of Stormwind Keep quietly let it be known that a diplomat sent to meet Lady Jaina Proudmoore in Theramore had never arrived, and that he suspected the Defias. His old friend Jorgen, in the Valley of Heroes, passed the matter to Elling Trias, a cheesemonger in the Trade District with a remarkable range of contacts.

The trail ran through a Defias gathering at Addle's Stead in Duskwood and a brawler in Old Town called Dashel Stonefist, then north to Menethil Harbor, where a retired agent turned barman pointed out a man listening too closely. Tapoke Jahn, called Slim, gave up the rest once he had been beaten. The Defias had not taken a diplomat. They had taken the king, on his way to a secret meeting in Theramore. Slim's part had been to get their man aboard the king's ship in the harbour: a Private Hendel, one of Theramore's own soldiers. And the Defias, he said, were working with someone bigger than themselves.

Hendel was found on guard duty in the marsh, outside Theramore, and taken back to the isle to be questioned. That is where the account ends. The king has not been found, and whoever the Defias were working with has not been named. Stormwind still has a boy on the throne and a regent beside him.
