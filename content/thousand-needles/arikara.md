Hagar Lightninghoof of Freewind Post wanted a look at an egg that the serpents of the canyons were guarding as though it were one of their own. When at last it was brought to him he took one look and sent for Motega Firemane at Whitereach Post. Vengeance had come to Thousand Needles, Hagar said, and Motega agreed: the legend of Arikara was true.

Arikara is not tracked but called. The way is the sacred fire of life, long dormant at Darkcloud Pinnacle on the isolated needle where the dead are buried. It is lit with a powder of Incendia agave, a herb that grows nowhere but the Needles. Motega sent the herbs to Magatha Grimtotem on the Elder Rise in Thunder Bluff to be enchanted, and Magatha gave a reason for haste. Too much of the tauren lore had changed, she said, and she feared it had angered Arikara, and that the serpent was stalking Cairne Bloodhoof.

Motega could not make sense of it when the deed was done. Why would Arikara be hunting Cairne? But Magatha could not be wrong, he decided. She was, after all, the tauren's most powerful shaman. He shook his head over it, and paid the hunter all the same.
