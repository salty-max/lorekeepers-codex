Sargeras was once the Titans' champion. When demons began to slip out of the Twisting Nether into the worlds the Titans had ordered, the Pantheon chose its greatest warrior, a noble giant of molten bronze, to hunt them, and he did so for countless ages. He defeated the eredar, sorcerers who turned whole worlds into demons, and the nathrezim, who turned nations against one another, and he locked both away.

Their corruption stayed with him. He fell into a brooding despair, then into the belief that order itself was folly and chaos the only truth, and at last into hatred of the Titans themselves, whom he blamed for creation's failure. His bronze skin split open on a furnace of rage. He broke the prisons he had built and set the demons free, and they bowed to him.

From the eredar he chose two lieutenants: Kil'jaeden the Deceiver, to find the darkest races and bring them in, and Archimonde the Defiler, to lead the army. He called that army the Burning Legion. How many worlds it has burned since, the book in our library says, is still unclear. We know of one it has not managed to finish.
