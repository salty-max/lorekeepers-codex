Atiesh was the staff of the Guardians of Tirisfal, Medivh's in the end, and after his death it was broken and scattered. Its splinters were gathered from the necropolis of Naxxramas, where Kel'Thuzad sat on his throne holding what the bearer sought. Its base went into Ahn'Qiraj with the dwarf explorer Brann Bronzebeard, who came out again without it.

A staff that had carried a Guardian's power had carried Sargeras's too, as its last Guardian had, and the evil was still inside it. Anachronos of the bronze flight, by the Caverns of Time, sends whoever brings him the pieces to Stratholme, to the consecrated ground where the greatest knights of Lordaeron were murdered, to call out the thing within and destroy it.

Very few will ever hold it. The bronze dragon says that the one who does was always fated to. The bronze flight says that sort of thing a great deal.
