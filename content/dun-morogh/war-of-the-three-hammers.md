Some two and a half centuries ago, Ironforge belonged to three clans at once. The Bronzebeards, the Wildhammers and the Dark Irons lived under a single High King, Modimus Anvilmar, and when he died they could not agree on who should follow him.

The quarrel became a war under the mountain. The Bronzebeards held the city. The Wildhammers, under Thane Khardros, were driven out and raised their own fortress at Grim Batol, and the Dark Irons of Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan withdrew to the mountains of the south.

The Dark Irons were not finished. They marched on Ironforge and Grim Batol at once, and lost both battles. In his fury Thaurissan reached for a power far older than any clan and woke Ragnaros, the Lord of Fire. The south burned: the Searing Gorge and the Burning Steppes are what remained, and the Dark Irons have served the Firelord in Blackrock Mountain ever since.

The Wildhammers did not stay in Grim Batol either. The death of Queen Modgud, slain at its gates, left a darkness in the stone, and they made a new home at Aerie Peak in the Hinterlands. Ironforge still remembers which clan stayed.
