Under Ironforge lie the caverns of Old Ironforge, and in them a place the dwarves call the Hall of Thanes. The Dark Irons have found their way to it. A map taken from their spies at Ironband's Compound shows tunnels and old highways converging beneath the city, and Earthseer Farsen, a visitor from Aerie Peak, followed them down to the hall and to the Dark Iron commanding there, one Durgen Dirgehammer.

The hall's own dead are restless. Afadra Dunwall, a lorekeeper of Ironforge, asks travellers to lay the spirits of Anvilmar to rest, and one of them, Faldrim Anvilmar, seems to have kept an old grudge well past the grave. King Magni wants the commander's head. I should like the League to have a proper look at the place once the Dark Irons are out of it.
