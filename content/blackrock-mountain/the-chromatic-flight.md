Vaelastrasz, who meets travellers in Blackrock Spire as Vaelan, says that before his banishment Deathwing was trying to breed offspring of his own: half-breeds immune to the powers of the Aspects, and wholly under his control. He failed. His son Nefarian, to judge by what crawls about the upper Spire, has at least partly succeeded. The abominations came to light through Ysera, the Aspect of Dreams, and she would not let the dragons stand by.

Awbee, a young whelp of the blue flight held captive in the Spire, knows how they are made. Riders on black dragons, he says, fell on the nests by night and carried off whelps like him. He saw his own brothers and sisters taken for Nefarian's experiments, and it was their essence that went into the chromatic flight. Nefarian wanted the failures killed at once. Their keepers made sport of them first, and what was left went into the lava or to the legion waiting below.

What came of it can be met in the upper Spire and in the lair above it: whelps and dragonspawn in the colours of every flight, drakonids bred from each of the five, and Chromaggus, the greatest of them, deep in Blackwing Lair. The blood of all five flights went into this brood, and only the black flight had any say in it.
