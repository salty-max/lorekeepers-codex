The Valley of Bones is full of the dead: bones and bodies, the skeletons of the Twin Goliaths, and things walking that should lie still. Some necromantic power has soaked into the place, and the Magram, the first clan to brave it, have decided that the magic is theirs. Nobody, I notice, asked the dead. Those Magram who do not fight with blade or mallet are being taught to raise the dead, and they have Necrokhans now.

The Gelkis came after them. Some Gelkis followed the Magram into the valley and took up the same art. One prisoner calls this a twisted bid to defect, and the Gelkis have sentenced every deserter to exile. The Gelkis scout Nerlokh was sickened by something else. The skeletons walk on two legs, not four, so they are not centaur dead at all. They belong to whoever lay in that ground long before Desolace belonged to the centaurs. Nerlokh could not imagine how the Magram could turn their backs on Mother Theradras to raise those bones.

The Magram mean to take the valley, raise an army there and bring the other clans to their knees. Orgrul, who gives the orders in their camp, has taken to asking recruits whether they are as eager as he is to rule over all of Desolace.
