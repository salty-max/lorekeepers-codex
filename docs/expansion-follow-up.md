# Expansion review follow-up, 12 October 2026

Implements the review of `ed44d0e..82ba359`: 65 new pages and the prose
deepened alongside them. This is a targeted follow-up, not a new certification
of the entire codex. The earlier lore and voice ledgers remain historical
records of their own passes. The current source policy is CLAUDE.md's
12 October rule: any publication may tell the past through Vanilla, with
period texts taking precedence and Vanilla's unresolved mysteries left open.

## Taelan: the ending follows the quest

Taelan's full page now unlocks only on completing
[In Dreams, 5944](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=5944/in-dreams), including
completion before installing the addon. Targeting Taelan (1842) and finishing
[Scarlet Subterfuge, 5862](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=5862/scarlet-subterfuge)
no longer reveal his death before the escort begins.

Hearthglen still unlocks on arrival, but stops with Tirion trying to reach his
son. Tirion's own page already stops there. The outcome stays in the page
awarded after the player has seen it. Previously discovered pages are kept;
this does not erase a character's record.

The final pools contain 294 distinct creature identities. Each ID was checked
against Wowhead Classic's `nether.wowhead.com/classic/tooltip/npc/ID`, in
addition to the pinned CMaNGOS template and spawn data. CMaNGOS
`28ef6259c782928b08a8dc9cacf6bc02e64f2b29` is a community reconstruction, not an
official Blizzard archive. Quest/event creatures need not have a static spawn:
Lunaclaw, Arei, Magus Rimtori and the redeemed Eranikus are deliberate cases.
The exact retained IDs are in each source page's front matter.

The Council's branches are deliberate inclusions. Original WoW's
[The Power to Destroy, 5725](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=5725/the-power-to-destroy)
identifies the Searing Blade as a Council sect;
[Hidden Enemies, 5727](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=5727/hidden-enemies)
establishes Neeru's loyalties; and Athrikus's
[Journal Page, 5839](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=5839/journal-page)
identifies the Dark Strand. The [Argus Wake](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Argus_Wake)
and [Shadowsworn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Shadow_Council#World_of_Warcraft)
accounts place their original-world sects under the Council as well. This
does not include the Cabal, Kil'sorrow or any alternate-Draenor organisation.

Membership is not inferred from a database faction number or elf-shaped model
alone. In particular:

- [Shandris, 3936](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=3936/shandris-feathermoon)
  and [Azuregos, 6109](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=6109/azuregos) now
  award their factions' pages as well as their individual pages.
- [Braelyn, 4198](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Braelyn_Firehand) belongs to the
  blood elves and the Shadow Council; she does not award the remaining high
  elves' page. Haleh and Itharius are dragons, not high-elf encounters.
- [Astor Hadren, 6497](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=6497/astor-hadren)
  is a Syndicate courier hunted by the Deathstalkers, not a member. The
  [original Deathstalkers quest, 1886](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=1886/the-deathstalkers)
  establishes that distinction.
- Nefarian's coloured experimental whelps are not added to the free flights.
  Unused templates, debug creatures, pets and mixed-race generic NPC IDs are
  excluded. Myrokos's ambiguous elf identity is not decided from his model.
  A transformed Scourge sentinel does not count as meeting the living army.

Friendly NPC routes use talking/targeting; hostile creatures use kills. Neutral
creatures with friendly interactions and hostile encounters, such as Azuregos,
Banehollow and Tortheldrin, can deliberately have both rules.

## Two clients, one page

An individual unlock can now carry `[classic]` or `[forever]`, with the same
build-time selection as paragraphs. The compiler validates the tag, rejects
a tag contradicting a client-only page, and requires at least one effective
unlock on each client that receives the page. Scoped area rules resolve against
that client's available area data. Tags never reach the generated Lua.

Timbermaw's Azshara area (1216) and seven original Azshara creature IDs are
Classic-only. Forever uses that location for Blackmaw Hold; it must not award
the Timbermaw page from the reused area or original tribal IDs. Felwood's hold,
the other eight members and friendly reputation remain shared routes. This
does not invent a Forever Blackmaw history or assign unverified new members.
