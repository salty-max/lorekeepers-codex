# Codex page expansion research

Researched 11 October 2026. This is a proposed shortlist, not approved additions
to PLAN.md. No entries, unlocks or generated content were changed.

## Recommendation

Add depth to encounters the player already has. The strongest gaps are stories
behind familiar ships, monuments, wanderers and factions, rather than another
round of small settlements and caves. Start with the nine pages below, then
choose from the reserves only where a distinct story survives the overlap check.

Keep Forever's new stories in a separate research queue. Its official previews
establish useful subjects, but do not supply enough detail to write most pages.
An area name proves that a place exists; it does not establish its history.

## Coverage and boundaries

The current source contains 371 entries: 187 places, 54 factions, 50 figures,
40 histories, 21 creatures, 9 callings and 10 forewords. There are 358
Classic-eligible entries and 371 Forever-eligible entries; these totals include
the alternative forewords, not just the foreword a particular reader sees.
Thirteen entries are Forever-only, including the Skyborne foreword. The major
Classic zones and their best-known landmarks already have pages.

Every recommendation must earn its place under CLAUDE.md's three tests: a story
beyond the visible encounter, a clear moment of discovery, and period evidence.
The shared material stops at Vanilla, using the permitted sources published
before 13 November 2008. A later source describing an ancient event is still a
later source. Forever's own quests and books can establish its separate history.

The proposed unlocks use the existing engine. A book or plaque can be evidence
without being an unlock: the Library already preserves world texts, but Codex
pages do not currently unlock on a particular book or an item in a bag. Area
numbers below were checked against the repository's area tables. Quest texts
were checked in Classic references and the pinned database; proposed NPC lists
still need the normal Wowhead Classic and client verification before entry work.

## The first nine pages

The order favours strong encounters and fresh stories, rather than fame alone.
All nine have usable Vanilla evidence. Their shared eligibility remains subject
to checking whether Forever changed the relevant encounter or present situation.

### 1. The Scythe of Elune

This is the most conspicuous missing Classic story. Duskwood has pages for
Stalvan, Mor'Ladim and the Embalmer, while its worgen mystery has no equivalent.
The page would follow Velinde's use of the Scythe, her journey, and the evidence
around Roland's Doom. The conclusion should preserve the original uncertainty
about the missing weapon. It should leave the reader with a disturbing connection
between two forests, rather than solve a mystery Vanilla deliberately left open.

**Overlap:** [The Worgen](../content/peoples/worgen.md) explains the creatures;
this page explains the particular Duskwood story. Keep Arugal's separate work
out of the main narrative. Jitters and the Dark Riders belong only where the
original journal and quest evidence supports them.

**Evidence:** the original chain, especially [The Scythe of Elune,
1043](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=1043/the-scythe-of-elune) and
[Answered Questions, 1044](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=1044/answered-questions).
The latter is evidence, not a proposed Duskwood unlock: its turn-in is outside
the chapter. Before writing, also read Velinde's journal and Jitters' journal
in full. Exclude the later Goldrinn/Ralaar origin, Legion artifact account and
later identification of the weapon's bearers.

### 2. The Third Fleet

Make the wrecks a human story: Fitzsimmons' account, the Intrepid and Captain
Halyndor, a dangerous cargo, and the effort to release the cursed crew. The
Eye of Paleth gives the tragedy a specific object around which the page can
turn. Neither [the Wetlands](../content/wetlands/wetlands.md) nor
[Menethil Harbor](../content/wetlands/menethil-harbor.md) presently tells it.

PLAN.md lists the Lost Fleet among places not paged. This is a reason to
reconsider that decision, not a claim that every omitted place needs rescuing:
here a complete Vanilla quest chain supplies the missing story. Prefer one
history page over separate pages for each wreck, captain and relic.

**Evidence:** [The Third Fleet,
288](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=288/the-third-fleet), The Cursed Crew
(289), [Lifting the Curse,
290](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=290/lifting-the-curse),
[The Eye of Paleth,
292](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=292/the-eye-of-paleth), and
[Cleansing the Eye,
293](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=293/cleansing-the-eye).
Use the original quests, not the Cataclysm replacements. Quest 292 returns to
the Wetlands; 293 returns to Stormwind and should not reveal a Wetlands chapter.
The arrival route also makes this Alliance quest's history accessible to Horde
readers.

### 3. Rexxar

A particularly good figure page because the encounter is memorable even before
the player knows his name. Explain the half-ogre wanderer's bond with Misha,
his part in Durotar's founding and his loyalty to Thrall, alongside his choice
to live outside the cities he helped defend. His story provides a different
view of the new Horde from its rulers' pages.

**Overlap:** [Jaina](../content/dustwallow/jaina-proudmoore.md) already covers her
decision during Daelin's attack. Refer to Rexxar's part without retelling her
whole story. Desolace is the right chapter for the Vanilla encounter, rather
than unlocking a Durotar chapter from a wanderer far away.

**Evidence:** Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne's Founding of Durotar campaign;
*Cycle of Hatred* (2006), chapter 9, which explicitly recalls the founding and
his preference for the wild; and [The Champion of the Horde,
6567](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=6567/the-champion-of-the-horde), which
places him on the Desolace road. NPC 10182 is present in the pinned Classic
database; finish the usual NPC verification before implementation. Do not
import his TBC location, later clan reunion, or modern biographies' additions.

### 4. The Timbermaw

The general [Furbolgs](../content/peoples/furbolgs.md) page briefly mentions the
hold, but leaves a substantial faction story untold. Focus on the Timbermaw's
survival amid corrupted neighbours, their suspicion of outsiders and the trust
that makes diplomacy possible. Their appeals to both Magni and Thrall make this
especially suitable for the same book read by both factions.

**Evidence:** Timbermaw Ally (8460), Deadwood of the North (8461),
[The Root of All Evil,
8481](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=8481/the-root-of-all-evil), and the
two versions of The Brokering of Peace
([8484](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=8484/the-brokering-of-peace),
[8485](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=8485/the-brokering-of-peace)).
The quests support a possibility of peace, not the claim that all corruption
has been cured. Friendly reputation provides a sensible early unlock; the
exalted diplomacy is supporting history, not a requirement to read the page.
Distinguish Felwood's Timbermaw Hold from Azshara's Forever Blackmaw Hold.

### 5. The High Elves

[Quel'Danil Lodge](../content/hinterlands/queldanil-lodge.md) explains one
refuge. A people's page can explain the older separation from the night elves,
Dath'Remar's exile, Quel'Thalas and its relationship with humans, then the
survivors after the Scourge. That fills a real gap between the night elves,
human history and the scattered high-elf NPCs players meet.

**Evidence:** the Vanilla book *The Founding of Quel'Thalas* (object 175733,
pages 1854-1864 in the pinned database), Warcraft III's Quel'Thalas campaign,
and the original high-elf encounters. Verify a representative NPC list before
writing the front matter; do not unlock it only at Quel'Danil if it belongs
to Peoples and Powers. Keep Tirisfal's reported unease a reported mystery.
Exclude Kith'ix, the Silver Covenant, the restored Sunwell and modern political
developments. Do not treat every surviving elf as holding one allegiance.

### 6. The Guardians of Tirisfal

[Karazhan](../content/deadwind-pass/karazhan.md) already gives Medivh's betrayal,
death and return. The missing story is how a secret human-elven arrangement
concentrated immense power in one champion, why the Council controlled
succession, and how Aegwynn's decision exposed the institution to catastrophe.
That earns a page more clearly than three overlapping biographies for Medivh,
Aegwynn and the Guardians.

**Evidence:** the Vanilla books *The Guardians of Tirisfal* (175735,
pages 1876-1891), *Aegwynn and the Dragon Hunt* (175738, pages 1906-1913),
*The Last Guardian* (175740, pages 1932-1941), and the novel *The Last Guardian*
(2001). The book transcripts establish the institution and succession. The
novel is the source to consult for any more detailed character scenes.
Keep this historical; do not import later reconstituted councils or Guardian
successors. An outdoor Karazhan/Cellar unlock avoids reliance on a later raid.

### 7. The Sons of Lothar

Five statues are an excellent invitation to a compact history. Explain why
Khadgar, Turalyon, Alleria, Danath and Kurdran crossed the Dark Portal, and why
their names are memorialised together. Finish with the uncertainty preserved
by the original inscriptions. This is a history anchored to a monument, not
an additional Stormwind district page.

**Evidence:** the five Vanilla plaques (objects 25329-25333; page texts
287-291), the Vanilla book *Beyond the Dark Portal* (21582, pages 633-636),
and Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal. The plaques explicitly say
"Presumed deceased"; the page must not turn that into confirmed death.
The matching 2008 novel is available within the permitted source list if
further detail is needed, but was not needed to establish this candidate.
Exclude later whereabouts and reunions. Use live Valley of Heroes area 1617,
not area 55, whose name includes UNUSED, or Forever's prototype area.

### 8. The Emerald Dream

Make this an explanation of a costly pact: Ysera's bond to Nordrassil, the
druids' long absences and the threat of something corrupting that realm.
That gives meaning to the sleepers and empty chambers the player encounters.
It should be understandable without a catalogue of cosmic planes.

**Overlap:** [Stormrage Barrow Dens](../content/moonglade/stormrage-barrow-dens.md)
is a place; [the Druids](../content/callings/druids.md) explains a calling;
[the Dragons of Nightmare](../content/peoples/dragons-of-nightmare.md) explains
the corrupted dragons. This page's subject is the realm and the pact, not
another account of those bosses.

**Evidence:** the Vanilla book *The World Tree and the Emerald Dream*
(175730, pages 1836-1843) and [Waking Legends,
8447](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=8447/waking-legends).
Use the book's period account of the spirit world. Do not identify the
Nightmare's ultimate author from later revelations, or import Freya's modern
creation account, Ardenweald or the current Dream's geography. A Forever
paragraph may become necessary once Hyjal's own texts are available.

### 9. The Shadow Council

[Jaedenar](../content/felwood/jaedenar.md) covers the present stronghold in two
paragraphs. The faction page can instead explain Gul'dan's hidden control of
the old Horde, its dealings with the Legion and the surviving organisation
behind cultists encountered across the world. Distinguish an organisation's
continuing name and purpose from an unchanged roster of its original members.

**Evidence:** the Vanilla book *Kil'jaeden and the Shadow Pact* (175741,
pages 1942-1950) explicitly describes Gul'dan founding a secret organisation
to manipulate the clans. The old Horde's Warcraft II account can supply
further history; the non-class
Jaedenar chain supplies the current encounter, particularly [A Final Blow,
5242](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=5242/a-final-blow).
That quest explicitly identifies Fel'dan's leadership and dealings with the
Legion. Check the actual Warcraft II passages before adding a detailed account
of the old Council's destruction. Avoid a warlock
class-quest unlock and claims about the Council's later Outland operations.

## Forever: the most useful next research

This queue builds on FOREVER.md; it does not re-propose the completed Zephras
pages, Hall of Thanes or Ruins of Lordaeron. Draft titles are questions to
investigate, not claims about stories whose texts have not been captured.

Blizzard's [Found Photos
recap](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24304071) places Forever
at the original world's early starting point. It names the Brotherhood of the
Horse and Powderfuse goblins in the Riverglades, describes the port's travel
connection, and discusses expanded Wetlands and centaur stories. That confirms
subjects, not their origins, leaders or motives.

The [What's Next
recap](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24303862/world-of-warcraft-forever-whats-next-panel-recap)
identifies Hyjal's aftermath and restoration, and Shen'dralas' connections to
the Shen'dralar, Eldre'Thalas and centaur. Use those connections to direct
research; do not fill gaps with Cataclysm's stories.

| Research subject | Possible Codex addition | Evidence needed before writing |
|---|---|---|
| Brotherhood of the Horse | A faction page if its history explains the Riverglades conflict | Full quest and gossip texts identifying the group, its aims and its connection to older history. The name alone does not establish any particular human order's survival. |
| Powderfuse Port and its goblins | A port page; a separate faction page only if it tells a different story | Port quests and dialogue establishing who runs it and what makes it distinct from Ratchet and Booty Bay. Avoid two pages repeating a trading-port overview. |
| Shen'dralas | The zone page, then a focused history connecting its Highborne and centaur stories | Actual local quests and books, checked against the existing Shen'dralar and Zaetar/Theradras pages. Check any changed account of the Shal'nan before sharing old paragraphs. |
| Nordrassil and the aftermath of Hyjal | A strong landmark/history page alongside the required new zone page | Local texts describing the tree's condition and the consequences of Archimonde's defeat. Do not import the Cataclysm Ragnaros/Firelands campaign or treat this as the TBC time-travel raid. |
| Gilneas behind the wall | A zone page and, if supported, the history of its isolation | Forever's own explanation of the ruins and inhabitants. Revisit the existing Greymane Wall paragraph at the same time; Cataclysm's worgen revolution is not a substitute. |
| Nightclaw druids | A potentially distinct Zephras faction page | The existing roadmap and area data supply a lead, not a complete story. Capture their own dialogue, quests and relationship to the other island groups. |
| Excavation Site: Wetlands, Blackmaw Hold, Shaper's Terrace | Individual dungeon histories where the revealed story earns a page | Quest texts, books and dialogue explaining the excavation, the hold or the terrace. Map names and boss objectives do not establish origins. Prefer verified quest turn-ins or arrival triggers over Forever instance kills. |

The best first capture would be a complete Riverglades faction chain, or the
Shen'dralas books and quests. Either could establish several connected pages
with more confidence than collecting isolated names across every new zone.

## What I would expand or leave alone

- **Grom Hellscream:** Demon Fall Canyon already tells his decisive sacrifice.
  A new biography needs a genuinely different subject, not the same ending.
- **The Silver Hand and the Kirin Tor:** their founding and roles already run
  through the Paladins, Church, Uther, Tirion, Mages and Dalaran pages. Improve
  connections or a missing paragraph before creating another overview.
- **Taelan Fordring:** Hearthglen and Tirion already tell the tragedy. Another
  page would divide a story the reader can currently understand together.
- **Deathwing and the Demon Soul:** the black and red dragonflight pages and
  Grim Batol already contain the core story. An additional page needs an
  independently useful angle, not a catalogue of famous names.
- **The War of the Ancients:** the Sundering and related pages already cover
  its broad history. A new page should illuminate a particular encounter.
- **Generic caves, small camps and ordinary rares:** keep the existing
  exclusions. A thin chapter is preferable to padding it with descriptions.
- **Riddles and treasure hunts:** these moved to the Field Journal. They do
  not belong in this expansion of the Codex's lore pages.

## Writing order and acceptance

Write the Scythe, Third Fleet and Rexxar first: three different experiences
with strong period stories. Follow with the Timbermaw and High Elves, then
the Guardians, Sons of Lothar, Dream and Shadow Council. Reassess the reserves
after reading those additions beside their neighbouring pages.

For each accepted page, retain a short evidence note with the period source,
its specific passage or quest, the proposed unlock and anything deliberately
left unknown. Verify IDs and the encounter in the intended client. Ensure
zone chapters do not open from remote quest turn-ins, and check that the
other faction can encounter a shared story where appropriate. Keep the
entry to the usual two to four flowing paragraphs; the research should make
the page more precise, not make it longer.

Compare the new page with the entries named under overlap. A reader should
learn something substantial from each, without receiving the same account
twice. Only then update PLAN.md, rebuild the generated files and run the
normal checks. No engine changes are needed for the first batch's proposed
types of trigger.

## Source notes

Classic quest links above refer to their actual quest description and
completion texts, not player comments or later guides. The original books
and plaques were read in the repository's pinned
[CMaNGOS Classic database snapshot](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/blob/28ef6259c782928b08a8dc9cacf6bc02e64f2b29/Full_DB/ClassicDB_1_12_1_z2815.sql.gz).
It is a community reconstruction, not an official historical archive; the
object/page numbers above make the checked passages reproducible. Further
character scenes still require their cited game or novel passages.

Rexxar's chapter was checked in a third-party digital reproduction of
[*Cycle of Hatred*](https://public.ds003.info/wcbooks/novels/World%20of%20Warcraft%20-%20%282006%29%20Cycle%20of%20Hatred%20-%20Keith%20R.%20A.%20DeCandido.PDF)
(chapter 9, PDF page 41). It supplies period text, but the reproduction is
not publisher-authenticated. No long quotations are reproduced here.

Some Warcraft Wiki and Blizzard pages refused direct retrieval. Search-indexed
official excerpts and the accessible Classic quest texts supplied the usable
web evidence. Wiki biographies were used to locate leads, not to establish
modern claims as Vanilla facts. Forever's preview material is sufficient for
this research queue; it is not being represented as a full quest-text audit.
