# Pages to write, per zone

The editorial line is in CLAUDE.md ("What deserves a page"). A zone gets as
many pages as it has stories: a small zone eight or ten, a large one like the
Barrens thirty or more. Sources: the zone's sub-zones from the game's area
table (`data/areas.json`), its creatures from Wowhead Classic (ids checked
before writing), lore from the Warcraft Wiki, vanilla era only.

Status: ✅ written · 📝 to write · ❓ needs checking before writing · ✗ dropped.

Left out on purpose: Season of Discovery additions (creature ids above
200 000), creatures Wowhead misfiles in a zone (Hogger in Dun Morogh, level 50
elites in Loch Modan), and anything failing the three tests.
