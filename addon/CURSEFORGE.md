Lorekeeper's Codex adds a book of Azeroth's lore that fills in as you explore. Enter a zone, speak to someone important or finish a quest, and you might unlock a page about the history behind it. The pages are notes from Archesonus, Royal Historian of Ironforge. On the Horde side, your copy comes from Parqual Fintallas, a historian of the Undercity, who adds notes of his own in the margins.

For Classic Era (including Hardcore and Season of Discovery), TBC Anniversary and World of Warcraft: Forever.

## What you can discover

The Codex contains **416 original pages across 44 chapters**, or **428 pages across 45 chapters on Forever**. It covers the original world's zones, landmarks, notable figures, peoples and history, from the Titans and the Sundering to the gates of Ahn'Qiraj.

- **Places and people.** Learn the stories behind the lands you cross and the characters you meet.
- **Peoples and Powers.** Pages on the playable races, the Scourge, the Defias, the Dark Irons, the dragonflights and many others. Your own race's page is available from the start.
- **The Callings.** The background of each class. You begin with your own and discover the others by meeting players of those classes.
- **A foreword for your race.** Each race gets its own introduction, including the Skyborne on Forever.
- **Ages Past.** Historical pages connected to the places and stories you encounter.

The lore covers the world's past up to the original game. It draws on Warcraft I to III, WoW's quests and books, and the novels and histories about that period. Later expansion events and answers to mysteries left open in the original game are kept out.

## Finding pages

Pages unlock as you play. Exploring, talking to or targeting characters, defeating creatures, completing quests and gaining reputation can all add to the book. Reading a history book or a monument's inscription can unlock a related lore page too.

A new page brings a chat link, an alert with its picture and a sound. Each character keeps a separate codex, with the date, level and location of every discovery. Tooltips point out creatures and players who can unlock a page.

Type `/codex` or click the minimap button to read. Browse by chapter, search your discovered pages or filter by type, such as places, figures or histories. Related pages link to each other. Only the pages and chapters you have discovered are shown.

## Keep the books you find

The **Library** saves the books, notes, quest letters and plaques you read in the world. You can return to them later, even after handing in a quest letter or leaving the place where you found a book.

Texts are copied in full and sorted into shelves, with when and where you read them. The Codex briefly hides the game's reader while it copies all the pages. Each character has its own collection. Letters written by other players are never copied. The Library and reading-based unlocks recognise texts in every game language.

## Achievements

The third tab tracks achievements for discovering pages, building your Library, completing chapters and meeting players of different races and classes. There are also feats for finding legendary wanderers, meeting your faction's leaders, meeting the historian behind your codex and exploring dungeons. Each achievement records the day and level you earned it, and shows in the game's achievement alert.

Open the tab with `/codex achievements`.

## Compatibility

One download covers Classic Era, Hardcore, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary and World of Warcraft: Forever.

Forever includes a Zephras Isle chapter and pages about its new dungeons. Kill-based unlocks work in the open world. Inside Forever dungeons and raids, the game hides creatures from addons, so kills there cannot unlock pages.

The Codex speaks English, French and German. With a French or German client, every page, chapter and window is in that language, with every name as the game itself writes it. With a French client, the books you keep in the Library also read in the game's own French. The German is a first translation: corrections are welcome on GitHub. Other clients get English.

If you want to help with future Forever chapters, `/codex scan on` records the places, creatures and quest texts you encounter.

## Settings and commands

A welcome page introduces the Codex on each character's first login. Use `/codex welcome` to see it again.

Settings are under **Options > AddOns > Lorekeeper's Codex**, through `/codex settings` or by right-clicking the minimap button. Adjust the alerts for new pages and for achievements (each its own switch), their duration, sounds, tooltip hints and the minimap button. Chat lines always show.

Each character has its own settings. Copy another character's settings within the same game using the welcome or settings page, or transfer them with `/codex export` and `/codex import CODE`.

- `/codex achievements` opens the achievement tab.
- `/codex minimap` shows or hides the minimap button.
- `/codex reset` clears this character's codex after confirmation.
- `/codex where` prints your position, useful when reporting a discovery or helping with new pages.

## Other addons

These share the Codex's book style and work independently:

- **[Hearthtale](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/hearthtale)** ([Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/E6gzPnN1)) writes a diary for your character as you play, with an epitaph when a Hardcore life ends.
- **[Yonder](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/yonder)** ([Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/ZKbq5y61)) offers exploration riddles, rare hunts, a bestiary, an atlas and records of your fish and herbs.
